About What If AI and The Register

How this site works

What If AI

Results come from fixed matching rules set by the Library. No AI system reads your answers or generates your results, and your answers stay in this browser.

Your answers are compared with the details recorded for each use-case idea: the task, field, scale, level, and setting. A closer match ranks higher, and any limits you choose, such as no AI tool for students, narrow the list. Results are grouped by how closely they match, and a card notes anything that differs from your answers or that you should check. The same answers always return the same results.

The Register

Every entry is a fixed, static record you can read, search, and print; nothing is generated as you browse.

Search looks for the words you type in those records, here in your browser.

What leaves your browser

Your answers, searches, and saved use-case ideas are not sent anywhere. The Library counts visits to these pages with Umami, a web analytics service that does not use cookies; it records which page was visited, not your answers or search terms. If you choose to answer the question at the end of a use-case idea, that answer is recorded with the visit. The full details are under “Privacy, saved selections, and site usage” at the bottom of the page.

Ask Us

During staffed hours, Ask Us connects you to library staff. At other times it shows a set menu of answers written by the Library and a form for leaving your email. No AI system writes the replies.

Links to other sites

Links to sources, and to AI products’ own pages such as those in The Register’s guide to types of AI systems, lead to separate sites that are not part of this one.

Where a source webpage has been verified, its link opens that page first. Known source-file links show a notice before you choose to open the file. External sites and your browser settings may still cause a download.

How it was made

AI assistance was used to realize this resource, utilized for coding, collation, editing, and certain visual elements. Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable, ChatGPT GPT-6 Astra, and Canva AI were employed in building the resource and followed a Human-Centered AI framework (HCAI) (opens in a new tab) and Human-in-the-Loop deployment (HITL) (opens in a new tab).
