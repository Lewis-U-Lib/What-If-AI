Privacy, saved selections, and site usage

Saved selections are kept in this browser. If browser storage is unavailable, the tools warn that the selection is temporary and offer printing or saving as a PDF.

This site uses Umami to measure page views. Page-view reports omit search text and activity links from the page address. When you submit an activity rating, the report includes the activity identifier, your selected response, which tool you used, and any follow-up choices.

Ask Us loads the Library’s LibAnswers chat service when you open it. The separate Send feedback link opens a Library form. Those services receive their own requests; the saved-selection notice does not describe their data handling.
