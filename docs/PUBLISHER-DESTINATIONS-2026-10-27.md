Every DOI received an individual search. Candidate publisher/repository pages were opened where found; gaps received source-specific title/DOI searches and follow-up retrievals. Matching used DOI, title, authors and version metadata. A returned page title or generic navigation alone was insufficient for the substantive-HTML category.

This pass identified official destinations independently; it did not capture a successful DOI resolver redirect chain for every record. It also did not test a person’s browser, cookies, institutional entitlement, or each server’s Content-Disposition header. HTML retrieval establishes a usable starting page here, not a guarantee that all downstream files open inline.

No reader paywall or permanent dead-link verdict was confirmed by this pass. That does not establish that every destination is free or working: 63 records lack a substantive official HTML record or retrieved full-text PDF. Retrieval refusals, empty extracts and unresolved records remain explicit in the CSV.

## Findings that affect the link decisions

The three Routledge parent books in this set are described in the publisher index as open-access editions under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; direct requests were blocked. Their commercial publisher domain is not evidence of a paywall.[1637][1640][1639]

TextGenEd’s publisher contents map the three generic book references to specific chapters. Their individual HTML pages were recovered; the Register citations should name the chapter authors and titles.[1594]

The exact AI Literacy for Researchers Zenodo version was recovered after following a latest-version link. It is no longer an unresolved version case. The implemented link is pinned to record 19483119; the moving latest-version route is retained only as retrieval evidence.[1606]

The HEA AI Literacy Training resource was verified as an official PDF. The official GenAI portal is an HTML starting point with a labelled link to that file.[181][1622]
