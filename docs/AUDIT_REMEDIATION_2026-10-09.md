## Additional improvements

- External links are checked at import and rendering. Executable schemes and malformed links cannot become activity, license, source, policy, product-example, or evaluation-guide anchors.
- Whitespace-only and non-text alternatives no longer count as usable routes without AI. The public release rejects non-text route values.
- The footer now explains local saved selections, page-view analytics, optional rating payloads, and the separate chat/feedback services. No claim is made about server-side retention, IP handling, or institutional approval.
- Both tools use the same no-payment rule. Institutional-access labels explicitly require checking local availability and approval.
- Each of the 150 recorded alternative routes now carries a public warning that its cost, materials, permission, and disclosure requirements are not independently verified by the main workflow's metadata. A complete review queue is included.
- Cold-load compressed-byte regression ceilings are 1.25 MB for What If AI and 1.45 MB for The Register. These are growth safeguards, not promises about loading time or a claim that performance work is finished.
- The three TextGenEd entries retain their separate IDs, activities, licenses, and umbrella citation, while linking directly to the January 2024, August 2025, and August 2026 releases.[9][10][11]
- The unavailable Reed syllabus has a dated access warning in Sources and in its dependent activity. No substitute or new reuse permission was inferred.
